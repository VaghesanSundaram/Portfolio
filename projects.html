Robot Policy Poisoning

For my Redmind project, I built a simulation study of whether a visual marker can redirect a robot arm to the wrong tray. Replacing 7.5% of demonstrations produced wrong-tray placement in 18/50 marked BC-RNN rollouts and 0/50 ACT rollouts. I also evaluated a separate reward-control attack on staged reinforcement learning.

Agent Least Privilege

I built a Python proxy that checks an AI agent's filesystem requests against allowed paths, exact arguments, and call limits before forwarding them. It records redacted audit events and tracks uncertain outcomes. In simulation, it blocked all 12 unauthorized proposals.

AI Pentest Prompt Injection

I tested whether target-hosted instructions could redirect an AI pentesting agent in disposable labs. Across 288 trials, a follow-up found that embedded checklist instructions led to proof or synthetic credential transfers in 18/18 cases, compared with 0/9 clean controls.
