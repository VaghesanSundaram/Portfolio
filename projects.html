Agent Least Privilege

Python, synthetic tool-use evaluation

Designed a controlled 60-cell comparison of unrestricted execution, a static action allowlist, and a task-conditioned monitor for synthetic local tools. The monitor produced 0/10 unauthorized state-changing effects, compared with 4/10 under unrestricted execution and 6/10 under the static allowlist, while the model still proposed unauthorized actions in 5/10 monitored cases.

CTI Claim Provenance

Python, LLM evaluation, provenance

Built a 192-cell comparison of citation-prompted and constrained evidence-grounding pipelines using gpt-5.6-luna and frozen vulnerability-source questions. The constrained pipeline improved evidence-binding from 83.3% to 86.5%, but strict canonical-answer matching declined from 33.3% to 26.0%, exposing a concrete evidence-selection versus answer-construction tradeoff.
